# Mortisomnia - A Minecraft Horror Mod

**Note:** This is an early stage of my unique horror mod idea. I develop this standalone, which
is really hard and time-consuming. If you are interested, give me feedback, support the project, 
or even help develop this mod!

### What is this?

Mortisomnia is **different** from the "typical" horror mod. 
No creatures popping out of nowhere, uncalled. The climax is not jumpscares with
clipping audio of screams.

**The goal is dread, not fear**. The dread of the world which has control over you.
This world is unholy, full of left over haunt, which targets you, the player. As you are playing,
you soon realize that **this world thinks**, and it does not welcome you.

Mortisomnia **coheres with Vanilla's style** in terms of art and
gameplay mechanics. It even attempts to improve it (e.g., giving more use to copper).

Mortisomnia relies heavily on the power of unknown, therefore not much will be revealed about 
the exact inner mechanics that make this an immersive experience.

---
### The Basic Lore

The world of the dead is connected to your world, and you increase this connection when
you kill or lurk in the dark.

When you kill, you often capture a part of the being's soul, a **soul-fragment**. This 
makes the bad spirits attack you more. 

You may choose to use the souls for you own advantage:
create items and weapons spiced with unholy, dark magic. You can also counteract the
otherworldly entities via the power of souls, but be warned: Practicing black magic is not a good habit.

---
### How this Works
Mortisomnia introduces the **haunt system**: a score that changes based on a player's actions.
Your haunt increases when:
- You lurk in the dark for long.
- You kill other entities.
- You... exist. 

Once you gather enough score, **paractivities** will occur. Paranormal activities that
this mod **think**, i.e., respond to your actions and surroundings sensibly. These 
manifestations reduce the tension you have built up with the world of the dead
(decreases you haunt score). These activities are scene-aware: they constantly analyze
the map around you to make the best out of it.
