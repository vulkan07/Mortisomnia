# Mortisomnia - a Minecraft Horror Mod

> Note: this is still in early development, functional gameplay is limited at best

Mortisomnia is built around a different concept of horror, 
that differs form the "typical" horror mods, like super loud and
scary looking entities that chase and kill you.

**The goal is dread**, not fear, not just jumpscares, but the dread of 
the unholy, paranormal word which has control over you. To make you feel
unsafe in your own world, fearing the unknown entities that act around you.

The world is a paranormal place now, haunted, cursed. It thinks.
It is observing you every second, every corner and the spirits
start acting once you cause enough disturbance.

The mod aims to integrate well into the **style of Vanilla** Minecraft in terms of art and
gameplay mechanics, even trying to improve it (e.g. giving more use to copper).
The activities more or less follow the design philosophy that distruction can only happen 
from consequences of the player's actions.

The mod heavily relies on the power of unknown, so not much will be revealed about the exact workings, events, and entities.
The basics idea, is the world of the dead is connected to your world, and you increase this connection when
killing or being in the dark. This makes the bad spirits act on you, loosening the tension.
You can protect yourself by either avoiding these actions, or by involving black magic (using souls of the killed
to your advantage), however that would set you in for more trouble. 

So as you can see Mortisomnia tries to achieve some level of meaningful gameplay, but it's a horror mod
after all.
 
### Technical working
Mortisomnia introduces the **haunt system**, which is a score that
changes based on your actions. It increases by killing, mining, being
in the dark for long, being alone etc... resulting in more frequent
and more... aggressive events.

These so called *paractivities* happen based on conditions and random timing.
If they succeed, they remove a certain amount of haunt score from the
player. But they can also choose to harvest the souls of the killed,
and used it for their own power, black magic. This would indeed increase
their haunt score.


### Plans, Considerations

- For now, the paranormal activities only affect the overworld
- For now, it works only in singleplayer, but is planned to work on multiplayer
- Only Fabric, 1.20+ will be supported
- Currently its visuals are tailored to Vanilla rendering, but supporting Iris shaders is also planned
(in the distant future)
- Content is constantly added, and tweaked until it hopefully achieves an immersive yet dreadful,
refreshing Minecraft experience.
